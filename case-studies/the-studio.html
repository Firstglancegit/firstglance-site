My work · Case study

The Studio, Biggleswade

A bungee fitness and activity studio on the High Street with 43 five-star Google reviews. Their old website was hard to change and hard to use on a phone. I built a new front end with Wix kept behind it, so bookings, payments and memberships carried on working.

FoundBooked
The brief

Keep what works, fix what customers see

The problem

A busy home page, eight menu buttons, a pop-up chat and an app banner customers couldn't close. Finding a class and booking it took too many taps on a phone.

What had to stay

Wix ran the bookings, payments, memberships and contact forms. Moving all that would have cost time and risked lost bookings, so it stayed exactly where it was.

What I built

A new, faster website in The Studio's own colours that reads the live timetable and plans from Wix, plus a rebuilt Google listing pointing people straight to it.

What changed

The details that make booking easier

  • Four clear menu items instead of eight: Bungee Fitness, Youth Sessions, Private Hire and Memberships.
  • A Book button on every page, always in the same place.
  • A step-by-step bungee journey from first induction to regular classes, with answers to the common questions.
  • "Limited spaces" instead of exact numbers, with "Only 4 left" shown when a class is nearly full.
  • Memberships grouped into adults, youth and outdoor education, so people find the right plan.
  • A party planner on Private Hire that sends the studio a ready-made enquiry.
  • A booking calendar that always shows at least eight upcoming dates.
  • Wix untouched behind it: the same bookings, payments, members and staff dashboard as before.
